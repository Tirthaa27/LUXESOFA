Our Story

Born from a
passion for craft

Luxe Sofa was founded by Tirthaa Tripathy in 2025 with a singular vision — to create furniture that transcends trends. Growing up surrounded by artisans in Odisha, Tirthaa developed an intimate understanding of materials, proportion, and the quiet luxury of something made precisely right.

What began as a single atelier has grown into India's most revered bespoke furniture house, while retaining the soul of a craftsman's studio. Every decision — from timber selection to the final stitch — passes through hands that care.

Our Philosophy

Sustainable Sourcing

We partner exclusively with FSC-certified timber suppliers and tanneries committed to regenerative practices. Beauty should never cost the earth.

Artisanal Mastery

Each frame is jointed without a single nail. Each cushion stitched with hand-knotted techniques passed down through three generations of craftsmen.

Designed to Last

We offer a lifetime structural warranty on every piece. Our sofas are engineered to be repaired, re-upholstered, and loved for generations.
